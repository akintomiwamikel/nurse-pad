Design a modern, premium, AI-powered web application called NURSE PAD.
NURSE PAD is an intelligent nursing writing platform that helps nursing students, nurses, researchers, and lecturers create high-quality nursing documents with AI assistance. It is NOT just an AI chatbot. It is a professional workspace that combines guided forms, AI writing, editing, document management, collaboration, subscriptions, and cloud storage.
The design should be clean, elegant, trustworthy, academic, and premium, comparable in quality to Notion, Grammarly, Canva, ChatGPT, Microsoft 365, and Google Docs.
The entire UI should be fully responsive for desktop, tablet, and mobile.
